About The Host

Organised by the Vaccine Theme at the Charité Center for Global Health

The Charité Center for Global Health (CCGH) brings together cross-disciplinary excellence to tackle pressing global health challenges. It's Vaccine Theme bridges discovery, clinical evaluation, real-world post-licensure assessment, and health policy frameworks to maximize immunization impact globally.

Vaccination in Pregnancy

Leading global research on maternal and neonatal immunisation through the international IMPRINT network to protect mothers and babies.

Modelling Impact & Value

Translating trial efficacy into population-level cost-effectiveness and indirect herd effects informing STIKO, JCVI, and WHO SAGE policies.

Translational Immunology (RCi3)

Harnessing the Research Center for Infection, Inflammation and Immunity to understand protective human immune responses for vaccine design.

Clinical Trials & Acceptance

Conducting multicentre GCP trials (MenAfriVac, Pneumosil, nOPV) with MRC Gambia alongside research into healthcare trust and vaccine uptake.

Host Department

Charité Center for Global Health

Fostering collaboration across the vaccine lifecycle.

A Dialogue Between Science, Industry & Global Policy

The Industry Dialogue aims to offer a neutral, scientific forum where academic leaders, global health specialists, and industry experts exchange breakthroughs in vaccine platforms, explore strategic public-private synergies, and debate policy and market access realities.

Historical Location

Hörsaalruine (Lecture Hall Ruin)

The Berliner Medizinhistorisches Museum's Hörsaalruine on the Campus Mitte is one of Berlin's most distinctive event venues. Originally the Rudolf Virchow lecture theatre it was destroyed during World War II. Its exposed brick and raw architectural character provide a vivid historical grounding for discussing the future vaccine innovation.

Address & Access Charitéplatz 1, 10117 Berlin (Access via Campus Mitte, Virchowweg 16)
